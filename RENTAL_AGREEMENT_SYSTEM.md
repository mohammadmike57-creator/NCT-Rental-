```
                                    ↓
                                 VOIDED
```

- ✅ **PENDING_SIGNATURE** - Awaiting customer signature
- ✅ **SIGNED** - Fully executed by both parties
- ✅ **ACTIVE** - Rental in progress
- ✅ **COMPLETED** - Rental finished, vehicle returned
- ✅ **VOIDED** - Agreement cancelled
- ✅ **CANCELLED** - Booking cancelled before pickup

## 🔧 **HOW TO USE THE SYSTEM**

### **Generate New Agreement:**

1. **Navigate to Reservations**
2. **Select Reservation** or click "New Agreement"
3. **Step 1 - Enter Customer Details:**
   - Full name, email, phone
   - Address information
   - Driver's license details
   - Rental period
4. **Step 2 - Vehicle Inspection:**
   - Select vehicle
   - Record odometer
   - Mark fuel level
   - Complete inspection checklist
   - Mark any existing damage
   - Take photos
5. **Step 3 - Terms & Pricing:**
   - Review pricing breakdown
   - Add extras (GPS, child seat, etc.)
   - Select insurance
   - Review terms & conditions
6. **Step 4 - Signatures:**
   - Customer reviews and signs
   - Agent verifies and signs
   - IP address recorded
   - Timestamp captured
7. **Complete & Generate:**
   - Agreement saved
   - PDF generated
   - Email sent to customer
   - Print physical copy
